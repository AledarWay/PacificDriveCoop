CoopSync for Pacific Drive - version history
============================================

Newest first. The installed mod's version is in files\VERSION.txt of the zip.


0.1.0-alpha22 (being prepared)
------------------------------
After the owner's alpha 21 test with a friend (host with an advanced save, guest with a new game).
The garage map:
- The guest's map table shows the host's junctions and roads. Before, a guest early in the story saw far fewer:
  the junction data were the host's, but the game hides what the player's own story has not reached. The host now
  sends what its map shows, and the guest's map draws that while it is in the lobby.
- The guest can no longer start a trip from the map by hovering a junction and holding the button (its game drove
  off alone), and cannot mark junctions for a scan there. Hovering still shows a junction's details.
The ARC map:
- The ARC map in the car points the same way for both. Its view takes its turn from the zone's north once, when it
  appears; the guest's had appeared with its own north before the host's came. The guest now turns it to the host's.
- A sky line from the host's next zone no longer turns the guest's north while the guest is still in the old one.
A guest left behind:
- When the host goes on to its next zone (or home) and the guest stays, the guest's sync stops in the old zone. Before,
  the guest took the host's new zone's events there and the game crashed soon after. Driving into an exit later, the
  guest follows the host to where it is.
The other's car and puppet:
- The other's car seen from outside moves smoothly again on two PCs. Its motion was predicted by the other PC's clock;
  when the two clocks differed, the car stood and jumped forward several times a second.
- The other player's puppet sits inside the car again, not outside by the driver's door. The seat was measured at the
  moment the game's own entry had attached the player to the car while still standing at the door.
The instability and the sky:
- The zone's instability storm comes for both. When the host's instability timer runs out (the siren, the walls
  closing in), the guest gets the same storm at the same place and the same ARC storm state. Before, each game ran
  its own timer and the guest's did not go off with the host's. The guest's own timer can no longer go off first:
  it follows the host's instability (the ARC shows the host's) and its storm comes only from the host.
- The sun and the fog follow the host's more closely: the host sends its sky 4 times a second instead of every 2 s.
  The fog and the sun swing back and forth with the weather, and the guest was up to 2 s behind (up to 10 % brighter
  or darker at a moment); now within about 1 %.
Abandoned cars' parts:
- The guest can saw and break an abandoned car's parts. Before, the guest's saw took the white bar and the red one
  never came, so the part never broke off: the guest's copies of the host's parts were never set up to break.
- The parts' damage is shared: both see the same white and red bars, whoever saws, and a part one player breaks off
  is gone for the other too. Before, each game had its own random health for every part.
- The abandoned cars' doors, panels, hood and trunk have the host's paint on the guest. Before, they all stood in
  the same orange-blue on the guest (only the body had the host's paint): the guest's copies of the parts were
  never painted.
Leaving together (the guest's drive-out and the next junction):
- The guest sees the game's own loading screen ("on the way...") instead of a black screen, from the moment it drives
  into the exit until it is in the host's zone and synced. The junction map no longer flashes up under it.
- The guest's world is paused while it loads to the host, and its controls are off: it can no longer walk about,
  see highlights or be attacked by anomalies under the screen. If the guest ends up in another zone than the host's,
  the screen lifts after 10 s instead of keeping the player blind.
- The guest follows the host into story zones (a stabilizer zone): the host told the guest the junction's ordinary
  zone while its game loaded the story's zone there, and the guest ended up somewhere else.
- Faster join: the host shares its zone as soon as it is ready (it waited 25 s every time before), and the guest
  drops its fixed waits (after the load, before the cleanup, at the garage exit) and imports faster under the
  loading screen. In the tests the guest is in the next zone about 13 s after the host's zone has loaded, instead
  of about 40 s.
The own car's back seat in the garage:
- The guest can get onto its own back seat in the garage (the back door only opened).
- On a back seat in the garage the key, the levers and the driver's door are out of reach, the way out is the back
  door, and the next time at the wheel is the driver's seat again (before, it put the player on the back seat).
The pause menu:
- Leaving the lobby (or the session ending) while the pause menu is open no longer leaves the menu stuck: Esc and
  Continue close it again.
Tests: the map test starts the guest from a new game's garage and checks the junctions, the roads and the blocked
trip; a new garage-seat test; the Esc test leaves the session with the menu open; a new instability test (the
host's storm on the guest); the light check in the next-zone test compares both games at the same moments; a new
part-break test (an abandoned car's parts sawed and broken off from both sides); the paint test also
compares the abandoned cars' parts' paint; the start-flow test checks that the host
shares its zone without a fixed wait.
Known issues:
- The anchor in both players' hands is still not reproduced in the tests (a stable anchor on a pedestal, the
  game's own long grab on the other side gives nobody an item). Please send a report right after it happens.
- The ARC map's north: both games have the same north in the tests, yet the owner saw the ARC maps turned 90 degrees
  against each other. Not reproduced yet.
- The instability glow on the guest's garage map can still differ a little from the host's.


0.1.0-alpha21 (2026-11-07)
--------------------------
After the owner's alpha 20 test with a friend (host and guest on two PCs).
Going to the zones together:
- The guest no longer gets the map to pick a junction: not when it drives out of its garage, not on arrival, not at
  a zone's exit gate. Its screen stays dark instead, as a loading screen.
- The guest goes to the host's own junction, in one travel (driving out of the garage it travelled five times at
  once, and its later junctions all led back to the host's first zone).
- The next zone together: the guest drives into the zone's exit gate and waits there, its screen dark; once the host
  has picked a junction and arrived, the guest follows it to the same zone (before, both picked on their own and could
  end up in different zones). Driving out of the gate again (4 s) plays on.
Cars:
- The other player's car moves smoothly as seen from outside (it moved in steps and jerked).
- A back seat can be taken through a doorway with no door in it.
- The driver's door can be aimed at, taken off and put on while the other player sits at that wheel (it could not
  even be aimed at); only its "get in" is off then. (Not yet checked by hand: the tests cover the getting in, not the
  taking off.)
- A door put back on the other player's car no longer makes doors fall off on both sides when that car is driven
  and jolts (the game's "door broke off" took the door of your OWN car).
- A part the other player threw down (a door taken off a car) falls to the ground on both sides (it stood in the air
  on the host and jumped up and down on the guest).
The zone:
- The orange instability spots around the junctions on the guest's map are the host's.
- Abandoned cars on the guest's side get the host's paint again when the game resets it (they all stood orange-blue
  again in the alpha 20 test).
- Hazards close to the guest and not to the host are run by the guest's game: a tourist mine the guest drove past
  went off on the host's side only.
Tests: a new test of going to the next zone together; the start test counts the guest's travels and checks that no
map is open; the anchor test checks that the other player gets no anchor in hand. The cars test caught a first try
at the driver's door fix that made the driver's doors of both cars fall off after a ride in the other's car (the
owner's item 9); fixed before this build.
Known issues:
- An anchor one player takes shows in both players' hands (not reproduced in the tests yet: please report how it
  happened).
- In one test run the guest had a second copy of its own car standing where it arrived, for the whole zone.
- The light can look brighter or darker on one PC: the time of day, sun, sky and fog are the same on both sides (the
  tests compare them), so this is likely each PC's own graphics settings.
Texts:
- The mod's texts are in one file per language (Mods\CoopSync\Scripts\lang\en.lua, ru.lua): a translation
  is added as a new file named after the game's language (de.lua, fr.lua ...); a language without a file, or a text
  a file lacks, shows in English.

  longer sees a zone of its own.
- The guest is in the host's zone faster: after loading the host's zone it no longer waits 25 s for a zone

- The guest no longer gets its car twice when driving out of the garage (the game's own trip and the mod's started

- Fixed: the guest's game could crash when going home from the zone (the sync kept working a few frames on the left
  zone's objects).
The garage and the pause menu:
- More space between the driver's checklist and the Co-op list; no ping in it any more (its updates made the driver's
  checklist fold and unfold).
- Pause menu, Co-op page: the lobby's state and the players are back in the column with the buttons, as text headers.
Cars:
- Getting out of the other player's car through a back door no longer leaves its front door standing open as well
  on your side (it opens for a moment for the walk-out, then shuts at once).
- A door whose seat the other player sits on is no longer hidden: it highlights, opens, shuts and can be taken off;
  only getting in there is refused. (The driver's door of a taken driver's seat stays hidden: the game's own way
  in there cannot be stopped.)
- The other player's car's dashboard shows that car's readings, not your own car's: the battery charge, the
  durability bar and the parts' condition on the computer screen.
- The other player's car no longer has its hood hanging in the air beside it (the hood of your side's copy of that
  car came off its place and moved along with the car).
The players:
- A kick at the other player now also takes 1 % of its health.
- Running over the other player: the driver now sees the hit at once - the other player is thrown as the car reaches
  it, and the car loses a little speed - instead of the car passing through and the player flying off a moment later.
  The player hit takes the driver's hit straight away.
The zone:
- Abandoned cars on the guest's side have the same paint as on the host's (they all stood in one orange-blue paint).
- The garage map: a junction's details (hazards, features, events) are the host's on the guest's side too; the
  guest's zone no longer gets random events of its own junction's (e.g. a radiation event the host did not have).
- The zone's gameplay events (storms, radiation and others, with their clouds on the map) are the host's on the
  guest's side: the guest no longer rolls events of its own, and the event's cloud is at the same place for both.
- The north is the host's on the guest's side: in zones where the game turns the north at random, the ARC map's and
  the compass's directions no longer differ between the players.
- Anomalies the guest's game spawned on its own during the run (seen: a pillar sticking out of the ground that the
  host did not have) are removed; the guest sees the host's anomalies only.
- Fixed an error on the host's side when an anomaly that moves vanished.
Known issues:
- The computer screen's overall status text and clock in the other player's car are still your own car's.
- The guest's game can crash now and then while it enters the host's zone or follows the host to the next one
  (seen twice in about 150 test runs).

- Tourists (the figures) stand in the same pose for both players, and one that sways after a kick or a bump sways

Texts:

Tests and reports:
